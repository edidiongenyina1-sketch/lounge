The Lounge

Built for the after-work
crowd of Owerri.

De Managers Court is where deals close over pepper soup and weekends stretch past midnight. Our kitchen cooks Imo classics the slow way, our bar mixes Nigerian flavours into proper cocktails, and the music is loud enough to feel and soft enough to talk over.

Come in for a quiet dinner, a birthday with sparklers, a match on the big screen, or a VIP table on a Friday night. Or stay home and let us bring it to you.

  • Kitchen

    Native pots and open grills

    Nkwobi, Isi Ewu, Ofe Owerri and point-and-kill catfish, next to asun and suya straight off the charcoal.

  • Bar

    Cocktails with a local accent

    Zobo, palm wine and Chapman worked into house signatures, plus full bottle service with ice and mixers.

  • Lounge

    VIP tables and shisha

    Private sections for groups, premium shisha, and a team that remembers how you like your drink.
