    About the Court

    De Managers Court is where Owerri's working crowd comes to unwind. Deals close over pepper soup, birthdays end with sparklers, and Fridays run until three.

    The kitchen cooks Imo classics the slow way: Nkwobi, Isi Ewu, Ofe Owerri and point-and-kill catfish, with asun and suya off the charcoal. The bar mixes zobo, palm wine and Chapman into house cocktails and runs full bottle service for VIP tables.
